Smart PM V1.5.4 - Machine-by-Machine Annual Review

Changes:
- Annual Plans no longer shows navigation buttons to Task Management / Multi-Year Planner.
- Annual plan is grouped by machine.
- Click a machine to expand its PM tasks on the same page.
- Click the same machine again to close it and mark that machine Reviewed (green).
- Review progress is shown as Reviewed Machines / Total Machines.
- Workflow Review Annual Plan is complete only when every machine with active PM tasks in that Line/Year has been reviewed.
- Opening Annual Plans alone no longer marks the annual plan reviewed.
- No PM scheduling, Running Hours, Excel, or Year Planner calculation logic changed.
