Automatic New-Year Rollover

The new year receives Machines, PM Tasks and planning rules only. Done/User/Execution Date are never copied from the previous year. Existing target years are never overwritten automatically.

For an old Annual Excel reference: import it once into a selected source year. After import, this planner reads its Machines, Tasks, frequency and planning information from System Master and can build any future year from it.
